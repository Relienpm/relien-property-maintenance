Common Questions

Membership, answered.

What if I don't use all my hours in a month?

Unused hours roll over to the next month, so a slow month isn't lost. If your plan ends up consistently larger than you need, we'll suggest moving you down a tier.

What if I need more work than my plan covers?

Anything beyond your monthly hours is billed at our standard hourly rate. Most members find a tier that fits their typical needs after the first few months.

Can I cancel?

Your first commitment is six months. After that, your membership continues month-to-month. Cancel anytime by emailing us with 30 days notice. No long lock-in, no penalty.

How are materials priced?

Transparently. We bill materials at our cost, with no markup, and we'll show you the receipts on request.

What isn't included?

Specialty trades like major HVAC repair or replacement, panel-level electrical, or major plumbing. Routine HVAC servicing is included on Basic and Premium plans; for the bigger jobs, we refer you to a trusted network of pros we vouch for.

Do I have to be home for visits?

Not at all. Most members aren't. We'll work out access details during your walkthrough and confirm your visits in advance.

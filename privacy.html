Privacy Policy

Effective: May 14, 2026

Relien Property Maintenance LLC ("Relien," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to information collected through our website at relienpm.com, our contact form, and SMS communications.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you submit our contact form, you provide:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • The message you send us about your property maintenance needs
  • Whether you consent to receive SMS messages from us

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, certain information is collected automatically by our hosting provider (Cloudflare), including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each
  • Referring website
  • Device and operating system information

This information is used to understand traffic patterns and maintain site security. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and provide a quote or consultation
  • Schedule and coordinate maintenance services
  • Follow up about ongoing or completed work
  • Send SMS communications, if you have consented (see Section 4)
  • Comply with our legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How Form Submissions Are Processed

Contact form submissions are processed through FormSubmit, a third-party form-handling service that forwards your message to our business email (info@relienpm.com). FormSubmit's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy, available at formsubmit.co/privacy. We retain emailed submissions in our inbox indefinitely for record-keeping purposes unless you request deletion.

4. SMS Messaging Terms & Consent

Opt-in

You may choose to receive SMS messages from Relien Property Maintenance LLC by checking the SMS consent box on our contact form and providing your phone number. Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Types of messages

If you opt in, you may receive SMS messages related to:

  • Responses to your inquiry
  • Scheduling and confirming appointments
  • Service updates while work is being performed
  • Follow-up after service completion

Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on the status of your inquiry or active service. You can expect typically 1–5 messages per inquiry or service engagement.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Relien does not charge for the messages themselves.

Opt-out

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message we send. After replying STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and no further SMS communications. To re-subscribe, reply START or submit a new contact form with the SMS box checked.

Help

For help with SMS, reply HELP to any message or email us at info@relienpm.com.

5. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies, limited to those required for basic site functionality and security (set by our hosting provider, Cloudflare). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; site functionality may be affected.

6. Data Sharing

We share your information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the site and respond to inquiries (Cloudflare for hosting, FormSubmit for form delivery). These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and only access information necessary to provide their service.
  • Licensed specialists in our trusted referral network, if you specifically request a referral or if your inquiry concerns work outside our scope. Only the minimum information needed to make the referral is shared, and only with your understanding.
  • Legal authorities when required by law, subpoena, or to protect our legal rights.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide, including secure transmission via HTTPS. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You provide information at your own risk.

8. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and related correspondence for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and document our work, typically up to 7 years for business records purposes. You may request deletion of your information at any time by emailing info@relienpm.com.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we have about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal record-keeping requirements)
  • Withdraw SMS consent at any time (see Section 4)
  • Opt out of any future marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@relienpm.com.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated "Effective" date. Material changes will be communicated through prominent notice on our website.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us:
